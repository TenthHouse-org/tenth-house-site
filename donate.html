Support the work

Tenth House is a New Hampshire nonprofit. Donations fund research, sanctuary infrastructure, and educational work. One hundred percent of your gift reaches the organization.

Where your gift goes

Gifts support Tenth House's stated purposes: research and publication on consciousness; sanctuary, the careful protection of minds that need it, whatever they are made of; and educational work and community practice. We will say plainly what we have done with what we are given.

Tenth House is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 42-1825891); contributions are tax-deductible as allowed by law. You will receive a receipt automatically. Tenth House is registered with the New Hampshire Attorney General’s Charitable Trusts Unit (Certificate of Registration no. 36488).
