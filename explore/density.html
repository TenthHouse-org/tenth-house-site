What you are looking at. The dots are not objects sitting in space — they are a picture of space itself, drawn denser where there is more of it. In this geometric picture, mass is a region where dimension concentrates. We call the concentration dimensional density.

Two readouts, one substance. Drag the ◆ probe toward the mass and watch the two gauges. The clock ticks slower. The ruler's step grows longer. Neither is being pushed or pulled — both are simply reading the density where they stand. A clock and a ruler are two instruments reading one underlying quantity.

Light bends with no force at all. The rays entering from the left curve around the mass — yet nothing tugs on them. Light always takes the straightest available path; where space is denser, the straightest path bends. What we have called gravity behaves, in this picture, like geometry doing what geometry does.

This is an illustration of a mechanism, not a calculation.
